# Open Questions

These questions must be answered before or during implementation to avoid incorrect content or workflows.

## Hospital Identity

- What is the exact official name format: Shree Shivaya Hospital, Shree Shivaya Hospitals, or another registered name?
- What city, area, and full address should be used?
- What are the official phone numbers?
- Is there a separate emergency phone number?
- What are the working hours?
- Is the hospital open 24/7?

## Services And Facilities

- Which departments and specialties are currently available?
- Is emergency care available 24/7?
- Is ambulance service available?
- Is ICU available?
- Is NICU available?
- Is maternity care available?
- Is pharmacy available in-house?
- Is diagnostic lab available in-house?
- Is X-Ray, sonography, CT, MRI, or other imaging available?
- Is parking available?
- Which amenities should be highlighted first?

## Doctors And Staff

- Which doctors should be listed on the website?
- What are their qualifications and specialties?
- Should consultation timings be shown?
- Are doctor photos available and approved for use?
- Should staff profiles include nurses/admin leadership or only doctors?

## Media Assets

- Do we have real hospital exterior photos?
- Do we have interior/facility photos?
- Do we have patient room photos?
- Do we have staff/doctor portraits?
- Are there any existing brand assets such as logo, colors, or brochures?
- Should stock imagery be used temporarily if real photos are unavailable?

## Appointment Workflow

- Who receives appointment requests?
- Should appointment requests trigger email notifications?
- Should appointment requests trigger WhatsApp notifications?
- Should patients receive a confirmation message?
- Are preferred time slots needed, or only preferred date?
- Should departments be selectable from a fixed list?
- What appointment statuses should admins use operationally?

## Blog Workflow

- Who will write blog content?
- Who approves medical accuracy before publishing?
- Should blog posts show author names?
- Should blog posts show doctor reviewers?
- Should blogs support Hindi, Gujarati, or another local language?
- Should comments be disabled? Initial recommendation: disable comments.

## Admin Access

- How many admin users are needed for launch?
- Should admins share one login or have separate accounts?
- Is role-based access needed later?
- Who can delete appointments or blog posts?

## Legal And Compliance

- Does the hospital require a privacy policy page?
- Does the hospital require terms/disclaimer text?
- What medical disclaimer should appear on blog posts?
- Are patient testimonials approved for public display?
- Are there any regulatory or advertising restrictions to follow?

## Launch Scope

- Which services must be present on day one?
- Which doctors must be present on day one?
- How many blog posts should be seeded before launch?
- Should the website launch in English only first?
- Is multi-language support a launch requirement or future enhancement?
